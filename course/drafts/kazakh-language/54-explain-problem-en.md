# Report a problem without guesses or blame

_Lead (summary):_ **You will describe an observable fault, clarify the conditions, and request a check.**

## Where we are on the map

This support map leads from a situation to a solution: notice the need or obstacle, choose a precise utterance, hear the reply, and check that both people understand the outcome alike. Trace the path with a finger, hide the map, and rebuild it from memory. Each node serves a real exchange rather than the memorisation of a rule name.

![Report a problem without guesses or blame](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-54-problem-en.svg)

## A real situation

A map link opens for one learner but shows a blank page for another. It would be premature to blame the file's author or claim the service is down for everyone.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Менің телефонымда бет ашылмай тұр.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-305.wav) — The page is not opening on my phone.

[Listen: Басқа телефонда ашыла ма?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-306.wav) — Does it open on another phone?

[Listen: Сілтемені қайта тексеріп көрейік.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-307.wav) — Let us check the link again.

[Listen: Мен қате туралы хабар көрдім.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-308.wav) — I saw an error message.

[Listen: Қандай хабар шықты?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-309.wav) — What message appeared?

[Listen: Скриншотты жеке жіберуге бола ма?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-310.wav) — May I send the screenshot privately?

## How to practise

First play the recording without reading the Kazakh line. Decide whether the speaker is requesting, replying, explaining, or confirming. Then uncover the line, repeat it as a whole after the speaker, and point out the word that changes the meaning. On the third pass, alter one condition and say your own line. If a partner understands it differently, ask for clarification; that is a communication skill.

## The precise model

A useful fault report has an observation, device or condition, check, and request. `Менің телефонымда` limits the claim to one device; `басқа телефонда ашыла ма?` checks the scope. Copy the error text accurately, but remove private details and access codes from a screenshot.

## The support signal

```kazakh
ҚАЙДА? → НЕ БАЙҚАЛДЫ? → ТЕКСЕРУ → КЕЛЕСІ ҚАДАМ
```

## The limit of the rule

`Ашылмай тұр` describes the current result, not its cause. ‘The service is broken’ is a stronger claim requiring another check. Never publish a screenshot with a name, phone number, or code.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the model, write your first response to the situation. Which facts are known and which are guesses? Choose an action supported by what you know. After the explanation, compare not only word endings but also whether the request or agreement kept its intended meaning.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the page. In one minute draw the four nodes, say a familiar line, the partner's reply, and your answer. Then change one fact such as time, place, or available object and travel through the path again without the example.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner says `сен сілтемені бұздың` although the link works elsewhere. Replace blame with observation and a question: `Менің телефонымда бет ашылмай тұр. Басқа телефонда ашыла ма?`

## Transfer to a new setting

Take another fault card: audio plays but the image is blank. State only observed facts and suggest one safe test.

## Change to the My World project

Add an anonymous fault entry to the project: condition, observation, test, result, and next step. Include no personal data.

## Exercise

Write a four-line report. Your partner then changes the device; narrow your claim to what the evidence still supports.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow rebuild the four nodes and speak a new two-line exchange without reading.
- After seven days solve an unseen task with a partner; they must restate your final plan in their own words.
- After thirty days return to the project card, change one condition, and repair an unclear utterance yourself.
