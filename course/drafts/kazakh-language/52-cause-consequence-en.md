# Why a plan changes: cause and consequence

_Lead (summary):_ **You will explain a change of plan, separating an observed cause from a proposed action.**

## Where we are on the map

This support map leads from a situation to a solution: notice the need or obstacle, choose a precise utterance, hear the reply, and check that both people understand the outcome alike. Trace the path with a finger, hide the map, and rebuild it from memory. Each node serves a real exchange rather than the memorisation of a rule name.

## A real situation

Before the presentation, the map fails to open on the shared computer. The team needs to know what was observed, why another format is needed, and what to do now.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Файл ашылмай тұр.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-293.wav) — The file is not opening.

[Listen: Себебі бұл компьютерде бағдарлама жоқ.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-294.wav) — Because this computer does not have the application.

[Listen: Сондықтан қағаз картаны көрсетеміз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-295.wav) — So we will show the paper map.

[Listen: Алдымен басқа файлды тексерейік.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-296.wav) — Let us check another file first.

[Listen: Мәселе неден шықты?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-297.wav) — What caused the problem?

[Listen: Әзірге себебі белгісіз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-298.wav) — The cause is still unknown.

## How to practise

First play the recording without reading the Kazakh line. Decide whether the speaker is requesting, replying, explaining, or confirming. Then uncover the line, repeat it as a whole after the speaker, and point out the word that changes the meaning. On the third pass, alter one condition and say your own line. If a partner understands it differently, ask for clarification; that is a communication skill.

## The precise model

`Себебі` introduces a cause: why did something happen? `Сондықтан` moves from an established cause to a consequence or action: what do we do as a result? Use three cards: observation → checked cause → solution. If the cause is unverified, say `әзірге себебі белгісіз`.

## The support signal

```kazakh
БАҚЫЛАУ → СЕБЕПТІ СҰРА → СЕБЕП → ШЕШІМ
```

## The limit of the rule

A missing application is one possible checked cause in this example. A file may fail for several reasons; do not assert one without checking.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the model, write your first response to the situation. Which facts are known and which are guesses? Choose an action supported by what you know. After the explanation, compare not only word endings but also whether the request or agreement kept its intended meaning.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the page. In one minute draw the four nodes, say a familiar line, the partner's reply, and your answer. Then change one fact such as time, place, or available object and travel through the path again without the example.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner writes `сондықтан бағдарлама жоқ` after the file fails, before checking the software. Replace it with a question and a check; only then state a consequence.

## Transfer to a new setting

New card: the paper map got wet, but the digital version opens. Separate observation from cause and offer a justified action.

## Change to the My World project

Add one change-log line: observed → checked → decided. Do not label a guess as a fact.

## Exercise

Build two causal chains from cards and spot one unverified cause. Say a sentence with `себебі` and a separate solution with `сондықтан`.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow rebuild the four nodes and speak a new two-line exchange without reading.
- After seven days solve an unseen task with a partner; they must restate your final plan in their own words.
- After thirty days return to the project card, change one condition, and repair an unclear utterance yourself.
