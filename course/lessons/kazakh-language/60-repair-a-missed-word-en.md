# Missed a word? Clarify instead of guessing

_Lead (summary):_ **You will pause a conversation, request a repeat and confirm what you heard in your own words.**

## A real situation

At a noisy bus stop, a partner gives the meeting location. You heard the time but missed the place. Guessing is riskier than asking politely for one word again.

## How to practise

First play the recordings with the transcript covered and state only the main meaning. On a second pass note anchor words; on a third, check the written lines. Do not let reading replace listening. Repeat the whole phrase at a natural pace instead of spelling every letter aloud. If the first pass leaves a gap, keep the main point and fill one missing detail on the next pass.

## The precise model

Repair has three moves: identify the gap (`соңғы сөзді`), hear the repeat, and restate time and place. `Мен сізді дұрыс түсіндім бе?` lets both people confirm the meaning.

## The support signal

```kazakh
ЕСТІМЕДІМ → ҚАЙТАЛАҢЫЗ → ҚАЙТА АЙТАМЫН → РАСТАҢЫЗ
```

## The limit of the rule

Ask for the part you missed rather than the whole story every time. An explicit check matters more than a polite nod.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the explanation, predict what the message is about and which fact your partner will need. Then compare your prediction with the recording and find one exact sound or word that changed your conclusion.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the text and map. Sketch four support nodes in one minute, say a line from memory, and tell a partner which conclusion follows from it and which would be a guess.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner misses `алдында` and records the meeting inside the library. Ask for the final word and correct the location.

## Transfer to a new setting

Transfer the repair to a phone call: you heard an address but missed the building number. Ask for that number, then repeat the full address.

## Change to the My World project

Add an audio-message card to My World 2.0: verified fact, unknown detail, follow-up question and confirmed answer. Use fictional names and locations. Add one missed word, a repair question and the finally confirmed address.

## Exercise

One learner hides the transcript and listens at normal volume; a partner masks one word with noise. Clarify only the gap, restate the message and wait for confirmation.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow listen once and reconstruct the four nodes without text.
- After seven days use the technique with a new recording containing a different time, place or object.
- After thirty days retell what you heard to someone else and ask them to check its accuracy.
