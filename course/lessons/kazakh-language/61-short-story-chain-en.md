# A four-frame story: cause, action and outcome

_Lead (summary):_ **You will hear a short story and recover both events and their causal link.**

## Where we are on the map

Trace the four nodes with a finger. Hide the diagram and recover only facts you actually heard. The colours mark message roles; they do not imply every sentence contains four special words.

![A four-frame story: cause, action and outcome](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-61-story-en.svg)

## A real situation

A paper plan goes missing while the group prepares its map. The team explains what happened and how it responded. Distinguish the cause of delay from the solution.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Кеше қағаз картаны таба алмадық.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-341.wav) — Yesterday we could not find the paper map.

[Listen: Сондықтан жұмысты кеш бастадық.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-342.wav) — So we started work late.

[Listen: Бір досымыз цифрлық картаны жіберді.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-343.wav) — A friend sent us a digital map.

[Listen: Содан кейін жолды белгіледік.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-344.wav) — Then we marked the route.

[Listen: Қазір карта дайын.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-345.wav) — Now the map is ready.

[Listen: Неге жұмысты кеш бастадыңыздар?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-346.wav) — Why did you start late?

## How to practise

First play the recordings with the transcript covered and state only the main meaning. On a second pass note anchor words; on a third, check the written lines. Do not let reading replace listening. Repeat the whole phrase at a natural pace instead of spelling every letter aloud. If the first pass leaves a gap, keep the main point and fill one missing detail on the next pass.

## The precise model

`Сондықтан` connects cause and consequence: no map → a late start. `Содан кейін` reports sequence: receive a file → mark the route. They are not interchangeable; sequence alone does not prove causation.

## The support signal

```kazakh
ҚИЫНДЫҚ → САЛДАР → КӨМЕК → НӘТИЖЕ
```

## The limit of the rule

Do not say the friend 'solved everything': the friend sent a file, but the team marked the route. Report only what the recording supports.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the explanation, predict what the message is about and which fact your partner will need. Then compare your prediction with the recording and find one exact sound or word that changed your conclusion.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the text and map. Sketch four support nodes in one minute, say a line from memory, and tell a partner which conclusion follows from it and which would be a guess.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner writes 'The digital map was late, so the paper one was lost.' The recording does not support that causal chain. Restore the actual arrows.

## Transfer to a new setting

A partner replaces the missing map with a broken projector. Build a fresh story with cause, consequence, help and result.

## Change to the My World project

Add an audio-message card to My World 2.0: verified fact, unknown detail, follow-up question and confirmed answer. Use fictional names and locations. Record a four-frame map story, using different arrows for causation and mere sequence.

## Exercise

Listen to the five lines as one story. Arrange four cards: difficulty, consequence, help and result. Change one fact, then tell the story again.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow listen once and reconstruct the four nodes without text.
- After seven days use the technique with a new recording containing a different time, place or object.
- After thirty days retell what you heard to someone else and ask them to check its accuracy.
