# Retell what you heard and ask the next question

_Lead (summary):_ **You will relay another person's message without inventing facts and ask about a gap.**

## Where we are on the map

Trace the four nodes with a finger. Hide the diagram and recover only facts you actually heard. The colours mark message roles; they do not imply every sentence contains four special words.

![Retell what you heard and ask the next question](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-63-retell-en.svg)

## A real situation

A classmate missed the project discussion. You know the time and place, but not who will bring the printout. A faithful retelling leaves that gap visible.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Ертең сағат үште кітапханада кездесеміз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-353.wav) — We meet at the library at three tomorrow.

[Listen: Алдымен картаны бірге қараймыз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-354.wav) — First we review the map together.

[Listen: Содан кейін бағытты таңдаймыз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-355.wav) — Then we choose a route.

[Listen: Баспа картаны кім әкеледі?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-356.wav) — Who will bring the printed map?

[Listen: Мен оны әлі білмеймін.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-357.wav) — I do not know that yet.

[Listen: Дұрыс түсіндім бе: кездесу сағат үште ме?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-358.wav) — Did I understand correctly: is the meeting at three?

## How to practise

First play the recordings with the transcript covered and state only the main meaning. On a second pass note anchor words; on a third, check the written lines. Do not let reading replace listening. Repeat the whole phrase at a natural pace instead of spelling every letter aloud. If the first pass leaves a gap, keep the main point and fill one missing detail on the next pass.

## The precise model

Keep the source and four anchors in a retelling: when, where, first action, next action. Do not turn `кім әкеледі?` into 'Aliya will bring it.' If no answer was given, say `әлі білмеймін` and ask a follow-up.

## The support signal

```kazakh
ДЕРЕККӨЗ → НАҚТЫ ДЕРЕК → БОС ОРЫН → СҰРАҚ
```

## The limit of the rule

A retelling may be shorter than the original, but must not change the agreed time or invent an answer to an open question.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the explanation, predict what the message is about and which fact your partner will need. Then compare your prediction with the recording and find one exact sound or word that changed your conclusion.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the text and map. Sketch four support nodes in one minute, say a line from memory, and tell a partner which conclusion follows from it and which would be a guess.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner retells it as 'Aliya brings the map at four tomorrow.' Find the two unsupported changes and correct the message.

## Transfer to a new setting

Receive a new message with an unanswered 'who?' field. Retell what is known and name the gap without assigning someone by guess.

## Change to the My World project

Add an audio-message card to My World 2.0: verified fact, unknown detail, follow-up question and confirmed answer. Use fictional names and locations. Create a faithful-retelling card with source, time, place, two actions and an open question.

## Exercise

One learner hears three lines, a second receives only the oral retelling, and a third compares it with the original. Rotate roles. Each round needs one question about an unknown fact.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow listen once and reconstruct the four nodes without text.
- After seven days use the technique with a new recording containing a different time, place or object.
- After thirty days retell what you heard to someone else and ask them to check its accuracy.
