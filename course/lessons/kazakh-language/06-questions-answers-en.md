## A real situation

Think of a question as an open slot in a familiar route. A statement contains a person, thing, or place; a question replaces the unknown part with a precise question word. A yes-or-no question has no missing slot, so a separate question particle appears at the end.

## The precise model

`Кім?` asks about a person, `не?` about a thing or idea, `қайда?` about a location, and `қайдан?` about a point of origin. Put the question word in the slot whose answer is unknown. Use a separate particle for yes or no: `Сіз оқушысыз ба?`, `Бұл мектеп пе?`. It follows harmony and the final sound of the preceding word, but it is always written separately.

## The support signal

```kazakh
КІМ? · НЕ? · ҚАЙДА? · ҚАЙДАН?  |  ИӘ/ЖОҚ → … БА/БЕ/ПА/ПЕ?
```

## The limit of the rule

Do not import an English auxiliary verb or rely only on Russian-style intonation. Rising intonation does not complete the written form, and the particle does not attach to the word. A full choice among `ма/ме, ба/бе, па/пе` requires the final sound. Today we use verified pairs and notice why they differ instead of guessing every variant.

## Predict before the explanation

Choose a question word for each answer: `Аружан`, `кітап`, `мектепте`, `Қостанайдан`. Then inspect `оқушысыз` and `мектеп` and predict why the finished questions use different particles, `ба` and `пе`.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the page and draw four empty slots labelled person, thing, place, and origin. Rebuild the Kazakh question words. Draw a separate yes/no switch and write two particles from the examples with a visible space before each.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner says `Сіз қайда Қостанайдансыз?`, mixing location with origin. Choose one unknown meaning and its matching word. In `мектеппе`, locate the boundary between the main word and particle and restore the space.

## Transfer to a new setting

Interview a fictional character. Ask about name, role, location, and origin, followed by one yes-or-no question. A partner may answer from cards. If a question needs a form not yet taught, simplify it to a frame from this lesson.

## Change to the My World project

Add five visitor questions to the project card: who the character is, what the place is, where it is, where the character is from, and whether one guess is correct. Leave a blank field under each for another learner's safe answer.

## Exercise

**Required.** Write four questions with different question words and one with a separate particle. Answer them as a fictional character in short complete lines.

**Transfer check.** Receive four new answers without their questions and reconstruct the question for each. Explain which unknown slot controlled your choice.
