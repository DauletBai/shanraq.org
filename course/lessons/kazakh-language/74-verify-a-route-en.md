# A route without guesses: verify each turn

_Lead (summary):_ **You will give a clear route and mark any segment that has not been checked.**

## Where we are on the map

Trace the four nodes of the support map with a finger and name the job of each one. Then hide it. Colours make the checking sequence visible; they do not stand in for evidence. If a node lacks information, leave it blank and explain why. These figures are invented practice data, not statistics from a real library.

![A route without guesses: verify each turn](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-74-route-en.svg)

## A real-life scene

For the exercise, the group draws a fictional route from a bus stop to a library. It has two turns and a crossing, but the crossing is unverified; it cannot be invented.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Аялдамадан тура жүріңіз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-419.wav) — Go straight from the stop.

[Listen: Бірінші көшеден оңға бұрылыңыз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-420.wav) — Turn right at the first street.

[Listen: Кітапхана сол жақта болады.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-421.wav) — The library will be on the left.

[Listen: Өткел қай жерде екенін тексерейік.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-422.wav) — Let us check where the crossing is.

[Listen: Бұл бөлік әлі тексерілген жоқ.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-423.wav) — This segment has not been checked yet.

[Listen: Басқа қауіпсіз жол бар ма?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-424.wav) — Is there another safe route?

## How to practise

First listen with the transcript hidden and state the main point even if you miss some words. On a second pass write only anchor words and numbers; on a third, open the text and find one error in your initial prediction. Repeat the full Kazakh sentence at a natural pace rather than spelling it out. If a detail remains unclear, use the repair question from lesson 60 and ask a partner to repeat just that part.

## The precise model

Use start → action → landmark → check. `Аялдамадан` tells where you start; `оңға` tells where to turn; `сол жақта` locates the destination. Give steps another person can follow. An unverified crossing cannot become a confident instruction.

## Support signal

```kazakh
БАСТАУ → ҚАДАМ → БАҒДАР → ТЕКСЕРУ
```

## Limit of the rule

The map is fictional, not real navigation advice. Claims about safety and accessibility need current on-site checks.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the model, write the conclusion you expect and what you can actually verify. Name one word or number that might change your answer. Compare your prediction with the evidence afterward; revising it is a sign of careful reading, not failure.

## Recall without the support

Hide the text and diagram. Sketch four nodes in one minute, say two connected Kazakh sentences, and explain to a partner where the known information ends. Reopen the page, compare every detail and repair one imprecise word.

## Find and repair the mistake

The directions jump from the stop to the library. Add the turn and mark the crossing as unverified.

## Transfer to a new scene

Your partner moves the start to a park. Does the first step still work? Make a new chain and name what needs checking.

## Change to the My World project

My World 2.1 remains a fictional learning guide without personal data. For every conclusion keep the original message, the date you checked it, and what you still do not know. Put only verified steps in the guide; leave a question marker at the unverified crossing.

## Exercise

Explain the route from the stop. When a partner points to a missing segment, ask how to check it instead of inventing it.

## Review after 1, 7 and 30 days

- Tomorrow listen to one line without text and recover its meaning and source.
- After seven days apply the same map to a new fictional notice or a fresh set of numbers.
- After thirty days explain your decision to someone else without the map and ask them to find an unsupported part.
