## A real situation

Before the project presentation, the room becomes unavailable. The organiser leaves a voice message with a new place, time and request. Pass only the confirmed plan to the group.

[Listen: Сондықтан кітапханадағы бөлмеде кездесеміз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-360.wav) — So we meet in the library room.

[Listen: Дұрыс түсіндім: сағат төртте кітапханада ма?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-364.wav) — Did I understand: four o'clock at the library?

## How to practise

First play the recordings with the transcript covered and state only the main meaning. On a second pass note anchor words; on a third, check the written lines. Do not let reading replace listening. Repeat the whole phrase at a natural pace instead of spelling every letter aloud. If the first pass leaves a gap, keep the main point and fill one missing detail on the next pass.

## The precise model

Separate the cause from the new plan: the large room is occupied, so another room is used. On the first listen name the change; on the second note place, time and item. If no room number is given, ask rather than inventing one. Relay one verified summary.

## The support signal

```kazakh
СЕБЕП → ЖАҢА ОРЫН → УАҚЫТ → РАСТАЛҒАН ІС
```

## The limit of the rule

This is a teaching checkpoint, not a real notice. Success means accurate place, time and action, plus an honest statement of anything still unknown.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the explanation, predict what the message is about and which fact your partner will need. Then compare your prediction with the recording and find one exact sound or word that changed your conclusion.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the text and map. Sketch four support nodes in one minute, say a line from memory, and tell a partner which conclusion follows from it and which would be a guess.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner announces 'the large room at three.' Identify two contradictions and ask a clarifying question about the room.

## Transfer to a new setting

An unfamiliar speaker changes the venue but keeps the time. Relay only verified changes and ask one follow-up question.

## Change to the My World project

Add an audio-message card to My World 2.0: verified fact, unknown detail, follow-up question and confirmed answer. Use fictional names and locations. Assemble project version 2.0: a voice message, four verified anchors and a separate 'still unknown' field.

## Exercise

Block assessment: listen twice without text, draw four boxes for cause, place, time and action, ask about the unknown room, and relay the plan. Repeat a week later with a new message and no support.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow listen once and reconstruct the four nodes without text.
- After seven days use the technique with a new recording containing a different time, place or object.
- After thirty days retell what you heard to someone else and ask them to check its accuracy.
