_Lead (summary):_ **You will name core parts of a home and locate a person or object by selecting the locative ending from harmony and the final sound.**

## A real situation

Someone is looking for a book. The answer `кітап` names it but does not help them find it. Add a coordinate: `Кітап үстелде` — the book is on the table.

## How to practise

Listen with the text hidden and catch only the target meaning. Next, trace the map with a finger and repeat after the model. On the third pass, hide the model, tap its short rhythm with your palm, and make a sentence of your own. The approved recording is a model; use a speaker, teacher, or comparison with your own recording to check your pronunciation.

## The precise model

The locative answers `қайда?` — where? The last vowel selects `а/е`; the final sound selects voiced `д` or voiceless `т`: `үй-де`, `бөлме-де`, `үстел-де`, but `шкаф-та`, `мектеп-те`. A Kazakh form does not have to encode English in, on, or at separately; the place noun and situation often provide the relation.

## The support signal

```kazakh
ҚАЙДА? → HARMONY: -ДА/-ДЕ → FINAL SOUND: Д OR Т → ҮЙДЕ · МЕКТЕПТЕ
```

## The limit of the rule

The locative alone does not express every spatial relation. Under, in front of, and between will later need relational words. Depending on context, `үйде` can mean in the house or at home.

## Predict before the explanation

Sort `үй`, `бөлме`, `үстел`, `шкаф`, and `мектеп` by а/е and д/т. Build each ending before listening and explain both signals.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the examples, draw a five-place plan, and move a token. A partner asks `Қайда?` on each move; answer with a complete short sentence.

## Find and repair the mistake

Repair `үйте`, `мектепде`, and `бөлмада`. The first two select the wrong suffix consonant; the third damages the stem and harmony. Preserve the word first, then select the ending.

## Transfer to a new setting

Hide an object in one of five safe locations and give a partner three spoken clues in Kazakh. The partner draws the coordinate without seeing the room.

## Change to the My World project

Add a fictional home plan with five labels and three movable objects. Make an audio route that asks a listener to place the icons correctly.

## Exercise

**Required.** Make ten answers to `Қайда?` with at least five locations and both д/т variants.

**Transfer check.** Give four coordinates from a new plan; a partner places the items and explains one ending error.
