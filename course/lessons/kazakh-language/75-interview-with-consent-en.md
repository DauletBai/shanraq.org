# Ask a person and respect their boundaries

_Lead (summary):_ **You will conduct a short guide interview without publishing an answer without consent.**

## Where we are on the map

Trace the four nodes of the support map with a finger and name the job of each one. Then hide it. Colours make the checking sequence visible; they do not stand in for evidence. If a node lacks information, leave it blank and explain why. These figures are invented practice data, not statistics from a real library.

![Ask a person and respect their boundaries](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-75-interview-en.svg)

## A real-life scene

A team member asks whether a fictional library is convenient. The interviewee may answer but decline to have their name or words published.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Бір сұрақ қоюыма бола ма?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-425.wav) — May I ask one question?

[Listen: Сізге не ыңғайлы болды?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-426.wav) — What worked well for you?

[Listen: Нені өзгерту керек деп ойлайсыз?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-427.wav) — What do you think should change?

[Listen: Жауабыңызды жазып алсам бола ма?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-428.wav) — May I write down your answer?

[Listen: Атыңызды жарияламаймыз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-429.wav) — We will not publish your name.

[Listen: Қаласаңыз, жауап бермеңіз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-430.wav) — You may choose not to answer.

## How to practise

First listen with the transcript hidden and state the main point even if you miss some words. On a second pass write only anchor words and numbers; on a third, open the text and find one error in your initial prediction. Repeat the full Kazakh sentence at a natural pace rather than spelling it out. If a detail remains unclear, use the repair question from lesson 60 and ask a partner to repeat just that part.

## The precise model

Keep asking, recording and publishing separate. Agreeing to talk is not agreeing to publication. `Бола ма?` politely asks permission; `қаласаңыз` leaves a choice. Use invented roles and collect no names or contact details in this exercise.

## Support signal

```kazakh
СҰРАУ → ТЫҢДАУ → РҰҚСАТ → ЖАРИЯЛАУ
```

## Limit of the rule

Do not present practice lines as real testimony. If consent is withdrawn, remove the answer from the public version.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the model, write the conclusion you expect and what you can actually verify. Name one word or number that might change your answer. Compare your prediction with the evidence afterward; revising it is a sign of careful reading, not failure.

## Recall without the support

Hide the text and diagram. Sketch four nodes in one minute, say two connected Kazakh sentences, and explain to a partner where the known information ends. Reopen the page, compare every detail and repair one imprecise word.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner asks “did you like everything?” then publishes the name. Ask neutrally and separate permission to record from permission to publish.

## Transfer to a new scene

A partner says, “You may note this, but do not publish it.” Show what stays in private notes and what stays out of the guide.

## Change to the My World project

My World 2.1 remains a fictional learning guide without personal data. For every conclusion keep the original message, the date you checked it, and what you still do not know. Add separate consent fields for taking notes and publishing; leave publication blank by default.

## Exercise

In pairs, conduct a two-question interview. Seek separate permission for notes and publication; leave the public card empty if consent is refused.

## Review after 1, 7 and 30 days

- Tomorrow listen to one line without text and recover its meaning and source.
- After seven days apply the same map to a new fictional notice or a fresh set of numbers.
- After thirty days explain your decision to someone else without the map and ask them to find an unsupported part.
