# Whose home is it? Building possession as a two-sided link

_Lead (summary):_ **You will build a “whose + what” link, distinguish my, respectful your, his or her, and a named person's item, and make both sides agree.**

## A real situation

Three books are on a table. Saying `кітап` is not enough to return them. Ask `Бұл кімнің кітабы?` — “Whose book is this?” — and connect the possessor and item with markings on both sides.

## How to practise

Listen with the text hidden and catch only the target meaning. Next, trace the map with a finger and repeat after the model. On the third pass, hide the model, tap its short rhythm with your palm, and make a sentence of your own. Device speech is a model; use a speaker, teacher, or comparison with your own recording as evidence of pronunciation.

## The precise model

Kazakh possession is like a bridge marked on both banks. The possessor takes `менің / сіздің / оның / Аружанның`, and the item takes the matching ending: `кітабым / кітабыңыз / кітабы / Аружанның кітабы`. The form is shorter after a vowel: `бөлме + м → бөлмем`, `бөлме + сі → бөлмесі`. A linking vowel appears after a consonant: `үй + ім → үйім`. Say the pairs as complete chunks first; use the ending chart to verify rather than guess at speed.

## The support signal

```kazakh
МЕНІҢ ↔ -ЫМ/-ІМ/-М | СІЗДІҢ ↔ -ЫҢЫЗ/-ІҢІЗ | ОНЫҢ/АРУЖАННЫҢ ↔ -Ы/-І/-СЫ/-СІ
```

## The limit of the rule

`Оның` means both his and her; context supplies gender. `Менің үй` leaves the bridge incomplete in a neutral full sentence, where `үйім` is required. A known possessor may be omitted in conversation, but the ending on the item remains meaningful.

## Predict before the explanation

Connect four possessor cards to four endings by colour. Before listening, predict first- and third-person forms of `үй`, `бөлме`, and `кітап`, then check whether a linking vowel is needed.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the chart and draw a bridge with a possessor on the left and an item on the right. Rebuild four forms of `кітап`, then replace the stem with `үй`. Say the question and answer without splitting a word.

## Find and repair the mistake

Repair `Бұл менің кітап` and `Бұл оның кітабым`. The first item has lost its possessor marking; the second bridge points to different persons on its two banks. Explain the meaning mismatch before selecting an ending.

## Transfer to a new setting

Find three harmless objects in a room and ask who owns them without assuming the answer. Then run a fictional lost-property desk using names and the respectful `сіздің` form.

## Change to the My World project

Give each fictional character one non-valuable object and a `Бұл — …` caption. Link possessor and ending by colour, then remove colour in a recall copy.

## Exercise

**Required.** Write eight `кімнің?` question-and-answer pairs using `менің`, `сіздің`, `оның`, and two fictional names, with stems ending in a vowel and a consonant.

**Transfer check.** A partner supplies a new noun. Verify its form in a dictionary, use it with two possessors, and explain the linking vowel.
