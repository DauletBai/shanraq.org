# First, then, finally: turning a list into a story

_Lead (summary):_ **You will connect actions with `алдымен`, `содан кейін`, `одан соң`, and `соңында` so that a listener can see their sequence.**

## Where we are on the map

The support map presents an action as a short route: the situation cue, a useful line, the other person's response, and a result check. Trace the arrows and state the meaning of each node, then hide the map and rebuild it on a blank sheet. Colour helps you find a step, but the response must eventually work without colour.

![First, then, finally: turning a list into a story](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-26-sequence-en.svg)

## A real situation

A friend receives shuffled cards for your preparation to leave home and cannot tell what comes first. You turn the actions into a route where moving one step changes the result.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Алдымен мен оянамын.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-138.wav) — First, I wake up.

[Listen: Содан кейін төсегімді жинаймын.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-139.wav) — After that, I make my bed.

[Listen: Одан соң киінемін.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-140.wav) — Then I get dressed.

[Listen: Сосын сөмкемді тексеремін.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-141.wav) — Next, I check my bag.

[Listen: Соңында үйден шығамын.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-142.wav) — Finally, I leave home.

[Listen: Бірінші не істейсіз?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-143.wav) — What do you do first?

## How to practise

On the first listen, hide the text and identify the place and the speaker's intention. On the second, reveal the Kazakh line, divide it into meaning units, and repeat after the recording without rushing. On the third, remove the model, change the time, product, service, or reason, and produce your own line. The recording models pronunciation; an appropriate response or action proves comprehension.

## The precise model

The connectors show an action's position in a chain rather than clock time. `Алдымен` opens the route; `содан кейін`, `одан соң`, and conversational `сосын` continue it; `соңында` closes it. Each verb remains the speaker's action: `оянамын`, `жинаймын`, `киінемін`, `тексеремін`, and `шығамын`.

## The support signal

```kazakh
АЛДЫМЕН → СОДАН КЕЙІН → ОДАН СОҢ / СОСЫН → СОҢЫНДА
```

## The limit of the rule

Sequence does not prove causation: ‘after that’ does not mean ‘because of that’. In a formal procedure, choose unambiguous ordering and numbering; in a spoken account, the variants prevent mechanical repetition. `Сосын` is natural in conversation but need not open every sentence.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the model, inspect the situation and choose the line that would actually complete the task. Record your prediction. After the explanation, identify the meaning cue rather than an isolated ending: habit, sequence, quantity, request, appointment time, symptom, or detected error.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the page and draw the four main nodes of the support map in sixty seconds. Use the drawing to produce the familiar line, one changed line, and an appropriate reply. If a step disappears, reveal only that area and rebuild the chain once more.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner begins with `соңында` and then inserts `алдымен`, so the listener loses the start. Arrange four cards from left to right and assign exactly one cue to each. Remove the connectors and ask the learner to recover them from position.

## Transfer to a new setting

Change the life task itself: another morning, purchase, dish, time, institution, or problem. A partner supplies one unseen card and checks whether you clarify its meaning instead of reciting the practised dialogue.

## Change to the My World project

Add four sequence arrows and one ‘if time is short’ branch to the daily timeline. The arrows must connect actual actions instead of decorating the page.

## Exercise

**Required.** Explain a new four-step process using four different connectors. **Comprehension check.** A partner deliberately swaps two steps; identify the error and restore a safe order.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow rebuild the support map and act out two lines without reading.
- After seven days complete a new everyday task and meet at least 7 of 10 criteria.
- After thirty days use the construction in a safe real setting and mark the support you no longer need.
