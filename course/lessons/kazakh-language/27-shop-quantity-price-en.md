# At a shop: product, quantity, price, and purchase check

_Lead (summary):_ **You will request the right product, state a quantity, understand a price, and check that you received your order.**

## Where we are on the map

The support map presents an action as a short route: the situation cue, a useful line, the other person's response, and a result check. Trace the arrows and state the meaning of each node, then hide the map and rebuild it on a blank sheet. Colour helps you find a step, but the response must eventually work without colour.

![At a shop: product, quantity, price, and purchase check](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-27-shop-en.svg)

## A real situation

Many products on a shelf look similar, and a price label is unclear. You need to name the product and measure, ask the price, hear the amount, and verify the purchase before paying.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Маған бір бөлке нан беріңізші.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-144.wav) — Please give me one loaf of bread.

[Listen: Бір келі алма керек.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-145.wav) — I need one kilogram of apples.

[Listen: Бұл қанша тұрады?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-146.wav) — How much does this cost?

[Listen: Ол сегіз жүз теңге тұрады.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-147.wav) — It costs eight hundred tenge.

[Listen: Барлығы қанша болды?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-148.wav) — How much is the total?

[Listen: Чекті беріңізші.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-149.wav) — Please give me the receipt.

## How to practise

On the first listen, hide the text and identify the place and the speaker's intention. On the second, reveal the Kazakh line, divide it into meaning units, and repeat after the recording without rushing. On the third, remove the model, change the time, product, service, or reason, and produce your own line. The recording models pronunciation; an appropriate response or action proves comprehension.

## The precise model

A request is organised around its goal: `маған` identifies the recipient, the product and quantity identify the item, and `беріңізші` supplies a polite action. `Керек` expresses a need without translating ‘I have’. Price answers `қанша тұрады?`; the total answers `барлығы қанша болды?`. Repeat the product, measure, and amount before payment.

## The support signal

```kazakh
КІМГЕ? маған → НЕ? нан → ҚАНША? бір бөлке → БАҒА → ЧЕК
```

## The limit of the rule

`Бір алма` is one apple, `бір келі алма` is a kilogram of apples, and `бір бөлке нан` is a loaf of bread. The unit changes the order. Lesson prices are examples, not current offers. Never say a PIN, confirmation code, or card details aloud.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the model, inspect the situation and choose the line that would actually complete the task. Record your prediction. After the explanation, identify the meaning cue rather than an isolated ending: habit, sequence, quantity, request, appointment time, symptom, or detected error.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the page and draw the four main nodes of the support map in sixty seconds. Use the drawing to produce the familiar line, one changed line, and an appropriate reply. If a step disappears, reveal only that area and rebuild the chain once more.

## Find and repair the mistake

A shopper says `бір алма` while expecting a kilogram. The seller correctly understands one item. Add the measure in `бір келі алма`, then ask the listener to repeat the order. If a label is unclear, ask instead of guessing the amount.

## Transfer to a new setting

Change the life task itself: another morning, purchase, dish, time, institution, or problem. A partner supplies one unseen card and checks whether you clarify its meaning instead of reciting the practised dialogue.

## Change to the My World project

Add a privacy-safe five-product list to the project: name, quantity, fictional price, and a receipt-check box. The total must equal the sum of its rows.

## Exercise

**Required.** Role-play buying three products and switch roles twice. State quantities, ask two prices, calculate the total, and request a receipt. **Transfer check.** Receive a product with an unseen unit and clarify it before payment.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow rebuild the support map and act out two lines without reading.
- After seven days complete a new everyday task and meet at least 7 of 10 criteria.
- After thirty days use the construction in a safe real setting and mark the support you no longer need.
