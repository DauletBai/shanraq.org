# Бұл кім? Talking about family accurately and respectfully

_Lead (summary):_ **You will name close relations, ask “Who is this?”, and answer in a complete frame without making assumptions about somebody else's family.**

## Where we are on the map

The support map shows one complete meaning link. Trace it with a finger, then rebuild the route without the picture.

![Бұл кім? Talking about family accurately and respectfully](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-10-family-en.svg)

## A real situation

Several people appear in a photograph. Instead of guessing, ask the neutral question `Бұл кім?` — “Who is this?” The owner of the photograph chooses the answer. We use a fictional family for practice, so nobody has to disclose personal information.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Бұл кім?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-045.wav) — Who is this?

[Listen: Бұл — менің анам.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-046.wav) — This is my mother.

[Listen: Бұл — менің әкем.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-047.wav) — This is my father.

[Listen: Бұл — менің әжем.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-048.wav) — This is my grandmother.

[Listen: Бұл — менің атам.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-049.wav) — This is my grandfather.

[Listen: Бұл — менің інім.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-050.wav) — This is my younger brother relative to the speaker.

## How to practise

Listen with the text hidden and catch only the target meaning. Next, trace the map with a finger and repeat after the model. On the third pass, hide the model, tap its short rhythm with your palm, and make a sentence of your own. The approved recording is a model; use a speaker, teacher, or comparison with your own recording to check your pronunciation.

## The precise model

`Бұл` points to the person or object in focus, while `кім?` asks about a person. A stable response has three cards: `Бұл` + `менің` + relationship. The terms `аға`, `әпке`, `іні`, `қарындас`, and `сіңлі` depend on the relative age and sometimes the gender of the speaker as well as the relative. They form a relationship map rather than one-to-one translations. Use `ана`, `әке`, `әже`, `ата`, and `бала` confidently today, and verify sibling terms from the speaker's position.

## The support signal

```kazakh
PICTURE → БҰЛ КІМ? → БҰЛ — МЕНІҢ АНАМ/ӘКЕМ/ӘЖЕМ/АТАМ
```

## The limit of the rule

In the beginner model, `кім?` is for people and `не?` for things and animals. Do not call an unfamiliar older person `ата` or `әже` merely from appearance; a kinship word expresses a relationship. The dash marks a useful pause in the diagram, not a separate spoken word.

## Predict before the explanation

Sort picture cards into person, object, and unknown. Choose `кім?` or `не?` for each, then predict which facts only the owner of the picture has the right to provide.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the list and draw a five-icon family map for a fictional character. Point, ask `Бұл кім?`, and answer. In the final round, a partner changes the order of the cards.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner asks `Бұл не?` about a grandmother and immediately labels an unknown person `Бұл менің әжем`. Repair the question to `кім?` and leave the answer to the owner of the image. Linguistic accuracy includes respect for the person.

## Transfer to a new setting

Replace the photograph with the audio description `Бұл — менің анам. Оның аты — Айгүл.` Draw only a privacy-safe relationship map and ask one clarifying question without requesting an address, age, or other private detail.

## Exercise

**Required.** Make an eight-line dialogue around a fictional family picture: three `Бұл кім?` questions, three different answers, one name clarification, and a polite close.

**Transfer check.** Receive a new family map, choose the appropriate younger-sibling term from the speaker's position, and explain the choice.
