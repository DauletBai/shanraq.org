# School as a map: places and clear coordinates

_Lead (summary):_ **You will name school spaces, ask where they are, and answer with the familiar -да/-де/-та/-те location coordinate.**

## Where we are on the map

The support is a route rather than a poster to memorise. Find the starting point, trace the arrows, and name the meaning of each step. Then hide the map and rebuild it on a blank sheet.

![School as a map: places and clear coordinates](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-17-school-map-en.svg)

## A real situation

You arrive at an unfamiliar school for an open lesson. A word list is not enough: you need to find the library, dining hall, gym, classroom, and the floor where the meeting begins.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Бұл — біздің мектебіміз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-085.wav) — This is our school.

[Listen: Мектепте кітапхана бар.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-086.wav) — There is a library in the school.

[Listen: Сынып екінші қабатта.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-087.wav) — The classroom is on the second floor.

[Listen: Асхана бірінші қабатта.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-088.wav) — The dining hall is on the first floor.

[Listen: Спортзал қайда?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-089.wav) — Where is the gym?

[Listen: Спортзал оң жақта.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-090.wav) — The gym is on the right.

## How to practise

On the first listen, hide the text and identify only the situation. On the second, reveal the Kazakh line, point to its meaning units, and repeat after the recording. On the third, remove the model, change a place, time, or direction card, and produce a new sentence. The recording supplies a pronunciation model; compare your speech with it or ask a live speaker to check it.

## The precise model

Name the whole scene first: `мектепте`. Then select the target: `кітапхана`, `асхана`, `спортзал`, or `сынып`. An answer to `қайда?` receives a coordinate such as `қабатта`, `оң жақта`, or `сол жақта`. `Біздің мектебіміз` also revisits the possession bridge: the place belongs to the ‘we’ group, so both sides carry a matching signal.

## The support signal

```kazakh
МЕКТЕП → ҚАЙДА? → мектепте / қабатта / оң жақта
```

## The limit of the rule

The locative ending marks a location, not movement. `Мектепте` means ‘at/in school’; movement towards school will later be `мектепке`. `Сынып` can name a room or a class group, so the situation must resolve the meaning.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the rule, inspect the new cards and choose a form by meaning: where an action happens, where a route starts, where it ends, when an event starts, or which rule applies. Record the prediction, then explain which signal confirmed or changed it.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the page and draw the support from memory in sixty seconds. Use it to say three lines: the familiar model, one changed line, and one entirely your own. If a link is missing, reveal only that link, hide the page again, and rebuild the whole chain.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner answers `Спортзал қайда?` with `спортзалға`. That gives a destination rather than a location. Restore the stationary coordinate with `спортзалда` or the natural answer `спортзал оң жақта`.

## Transfer to a new setting

Change the situation rather than replacing a single word: use another school, day, time, route, or rule. A partner supplies one unseen card and checks whether your ending still matches the intended meaning.

## Change to the My World project

Add a fictional five-room school plan to version 0.3. Do not publish real room numbers, staff names, or a child’s actual timetable.

## Exercise

**Required.** Draw a fictional school and give five coordinates. Ask two `қайда?` questions and make one answer negative with `жоқ`. **Transfer check.** Receive an unseen plan and locate three places in one minute without a script.

- After thirty days use the construction in a safe real setting and mark the support you no longer need.
