# To whom and where? Dative draws an arrow to a goal

_Lead (summary):_ **You will connect a recipient, destination, and appointed time through one image of movement towards a point.**

## Where we are on the map

The support shows a word's role in a scene rather than a table of endings. First find the image: actor, relationship, affected object, goal, location, source, or shared means. Only then trace the arrow to a question and form. Hide the labels and rebuild the route from the pictures.

![To whom and where? Dative draws an arrow to a goal](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-36-dative-en.svg)

## A real situation

A school volunteer project requires taking a map to a teacher, going to a library, and arriving for an appointed time. The three settings differ, but each has an endpoint for an arrow.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Мен кітапханаға барамын.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-197.wav) — I am going to the library.

[Listen: Картаны мұғалімге беремін.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-198.wav) — I give the map to the teacher.

[Listen: Досыма хабарлама жазамын.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-199.wav) — I write a message to my friend.

[Listen: Сағат үшке жазыламын.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-177.wav) — I book an appointment for three.

[Listen: Балалар алаңына дейін барамыз.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-201.wav) — We go as far as the playground.

[Listen: Сізге қалай көмектесемін?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-202.wav) — How can I help you?

## How to practise

On the first listen, hide the text and identify the role of the highlighted word. On the second, reveal the Kazakh line, point to the stem and ending, and repeat the whole sentence. On the third, replace the place, object, or participant and choose the role again before choosing an ending. Use the recording for pronunciation and prove understanding with a new example.

## The precise model

Dative answers `кімге/неге/қайда?` and leads to a recipient or goal. `-ға/-ге` appear after a vowel and voiced environment, while `-қа/-ке` follow a voiceless consonant: `кітапханаға`, `мұғалімге`, `алаңға`, and `мектепке`. Personal `сізге` and `досыма` identify the person towards whom an action is directed. An appointment time is also conceptualised as a point: `сағат үшке`.

## The support signal

```kazakh
БАСТАУ ─────→ КІМГЕ/ҚАЙДА? → кітапханаға / мұғалімге / сағат үшке
```

## The limit of the rule

Dative states a goal but does not report whether it was reached. `Кітапханаға барамын` is movement towards a library; being inside is `кітапханада`. `Дейін` marks an ‘as far as’ boundary and normally follows a dative form. Do not merge a message recipient with a specific affected object: `картаны мұғалімге беремін` contains two roles.

## Predict before the explanation

Before the explanation, act out the scene with objects. Ask who acts, whose part it is, what is specifically affected, to whom or where it goes, where it is, where it comes from, and with whom or by what it is done. Record the role and question, then predict the form. The rule checks your decision rather than replacing it.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the lesson and draw a three-object scene. Label roles without case names, then add questions and finally endings. Say the familiar sentence, a changed sentence, and your own. If a form disappears, return to the meaning arrow instead of mechanically trying every option.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner says `мұғалімді картаны беремін`: both nouns have become affected objects and the recipient has disappeared. Put the map in the sender's hand and draw an arrow to the teacher: `картаны` is what, while `мұғалімге` is to whom.

## Transfer to a new setting

Receive an unseen scene with another object, person, route, or means. Explain the form with one short reason: ‘this is the goal’, ‘this is the location’, or ‘this is the source’. A partner changes one role; change only the ending and meaning connected with that role.

## Change to the My World project

Add three arrows to the project: person-recipient, place-goal, and appointment time. Label each start and endpoint without using a child's real address.

## Exercise

**Required.** Build six lines with three kinds of goal and explain each endpoint. **Transfer check.** A partner turns the destination into a stationary location; replace dative with locative and explain the changed scene.
