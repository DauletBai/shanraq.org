# Read three bars without guessing

_Lead (summary):_ **You will state the period, unit and exact values before drawing a conclusion from a chart.**

## Where we are on the map

Trace the four nodes of the support map with a finger and name the job of each one. Then hide it. Colours make the checking sequence visible; they do not stand in for evidence. If a node lacks information, leave it blank and explain why. These figures are invented practice data, not statistics from a real library.

## A real-life scene

A fictional library gives the group a three-day practice table: Monday 12, Tuesday 18, Wednesday 15 visitors. These are invented exercise values. The group draws equal-width bars starting at zero.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Дүйсенбіде он екі адам келді.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-377.wav) — Twelve people came on Monday.

[Listen: Сейсенбіде он сегіз адам келді.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-378.wav) — Eighteen people came on Tuesday.

[Listen: Сәрсенбіде он бес адам келді.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-379.wav) — Fifteen people came on Wednesday.

[Listen: Өлшем бірлігі — адам саны.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-380.wav) — The unit is number of people.

[Listen: Ең биік баған сейсенбіге тиесілі.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-381.wav) — The tallest bar belongs to Tuesday.

[Listen: Бұл дерек тек үш күнді қамтиды.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-382.wav) — These data cover only three days.

## How to practise

First listen with the transcript hidden and state the main point even if you miss some words. On a second pass write only anchor words and numbers; on a third, open the text and find one error in your initial prediction. Repeat the full Kazakh sentence at a natural pace rather than spelling it out. If a detail remains unclear, use the repair question from lesson 60 and ask a partner to repeat just that part.

## The precise model

Read left to right: day label, bar height and the unit “people.” Say `12–18–15` before stating that Tuesday is highest among these three days. Use the same zero baseline: a truncated scale exaggerates the gap. Three days cannot describe the whole month.

## Support signal

```kazakh
КЕЗЕҢ → ӨЛШЕМ → МӘНДЕР → ШЕК
```

## Limit of the rule

A taller bar shows a larger count, not why it happened. We do not know whether Tuesday had a holiday, different weather or an event.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the model, write the conclusion you expect and what you can actually verify. Name one word or number that might change your answer. Compare your prediction with the evidence afterward; revising it is a sign of careful reading, not failure.

## Recall without the support

Hide the text and diagram. Sketch four nodes in one minute, say two connected Kazakh sentences, and explain to a partner where the known information ends. Reopen the page, compare every detail and repair one imprecise word.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner labels Wednesday 18 because its bar looks tall. Return to the source table and correct the label.

## Transfer to a new scene

A partner shows unlabeled bars. Which four questions must you ask before publishing the graphic?

## Change to the My World project

My World 2.1 remains a fictional learning guide without personal data. For every conclusion keep the original message, the date you checked it, and what you still do not know. Keep the practice table beside its chart; label it “fictional exercise data,” with dates and unit.

## Exercise

Draw a 0–20 scale in steps of five and bars for 12, 18 and 15. Label days and unit. With the table hidden, say the values and the limit of your conclusion.
