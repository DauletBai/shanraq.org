# One or several: choosing six plural endings as one system

_Lead (summary):_ **You will select -лар/-лер, -дар/-дер, or -тар/-тер from two signals: the word's vowel family and its final sound.**

## A real situation

A home inventory must distinguish one object from several. Instead of memorising six arbitrary tails, use two traffic lights: the last vowel selects а or е, and the last sound selects л, д, or т.

## How to practise

Listen with the text hidden and catch only the target meaning. Next, trace the map with a finger and repeat after the model. On the third pass, hide the model, tap its short rhythm with your palm, and make a sentence of your own. Device speech is a model; use a speaker, teacher, or comparison with your own recording as evidence of pronunciation.

## The precise model

First hear the final vowel: a back-vowel word selects `а`, and a front-vowel word selects `е`. Then inspect the final sound of the stem. A vowel and usually `й, р, у` select `л`: `бала-лар`, `үй-лер`. The sounds `л, м, н, ң, з, ж` select `д`: `адам-дар`, `гүл-дер`. A voiceless consonant selects `т`: `кітап-тар`, `есік-тер`. This is one route with two decisions rather than six disconnected rules.

## The support signal

```kazakh
FINAL VOWEL: А/Е → FINAL SOUND: Л/Д/Т → -ЛАР/-ЛЕР · -ДАР/-ДЕР · -ТАР/-ТЕР
```

## The limit of the rule

After a numeral, the noun normally remains singular: `үш бөлме`, not `үш бөлмелер`. Some nouns have collective or special uses, so verify context. Select by sound rather than by a look-alike letter from another language.

## Predict before the explanation

Send `үй`, `адам`, `кітап`, `бөлме`, `гүл`, and `есік` through the а/е lane and then the л/д/т gate. State each result before listening.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Draw the two traffic lights and rebuild all six forms. Hide the labels and explain each route aloud: final vowel, final sound, ending.

## Find and repair the mistake

Repair `кітапдар`, `адамлар`, and `бөлмалар`. Change only the decision that failed and name its signal. “It sounds better” is not a sufficient explanation.

## Transfer to a new setting

Receive six unfamiliar object cards, form their plurals, and verify them in a dictionary. Keep an error card with its explanation instead of deleting it.

## Change to the My World project

Mark groups in the home plan: `балалар`, `бөлмелер`, `гүлдер`, and `кітаптар`. Leave two small traffic lights beside them, then make a clean recall copy.

## Exercise

**Required.** Form the plural of twelve words, evenly covering the three initial consonants of the endings. Write both selection signals for every word.

**Transfer check.** Explain the forms of three new words and use each in a sentence without a numeral.
