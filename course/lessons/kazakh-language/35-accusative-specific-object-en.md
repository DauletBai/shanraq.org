# What exactly? Accusative and a visible affected object

_Lead (summary):_ **You will distinguish a general activity from an action involving a specific object already clear to the speakers.**

## A real situation

Several books are on a table. ‘I read books’ can state an activity in general, but pointing to the blue book requires you to show which exact item will be read, opened, or passed.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Мен кітап оқимын.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-016.wav) — I read a book or engage in reading.

[Listen: Мен бұл кітапты оқимын.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-192.wav) — I am reading this particular book.

[Listen: Көк дәптерді ашыңызшы.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-193.wav) — Please open the blue notebook.

[Listen: Картаны үстелге қойыңызшы.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-194.wav) — Please put the map on the table.

[Listen: Мен тапсырманы орындаймын.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-195.wav) — I complete the specific task.

[Listen: Қай кітапты таңдайсыз?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-196.wav) — Which book will you choose?

## How to practise

On the first listen, hide the text and identify the role of the highlighted word. On the second, reveal the Kazakh line, point to the stem and ending, and repeat the whole sentence. On the third, replace the place, object, or participant and choose the role again before choosing an ending. Use the recording for pronunciation and prove understanding with a new example.

## The precise model

A definite direct object answers `кімді/нені?` and takes `-ны/-ні`, `-ды/-ді`, or `-ты/-ті`, as in `кітапты`, `дәптерді`, and `тапсырманы`. A bare form may name an activity or an indefinite object: `кітап оқимын`. Demonstratives such as `бұл` and `мына`, context, and shared experience make an item specific and usually require accusative.

## The support signal

```kazakh
ЖАЛПЫ ІС → кітап оқимын | НАҚТЫ НЫСАН → бұл кітапты оқимын
```

## The limit of the rule

A missing ending is not automatically an error or indefinite meaning; some combinations form one established activity. Accusative is not translated by a separate word either. Check whether the speakers share one identifiable object. Use `кімді?` for people and `нені?` for things.

## Predict before the explanation

Before the explanation, act out the scene with objects. Ask who acts, whose part it is, what is specifically affected, to whom or where it goes, where it is, where it comes from, and with whom or by what it is done. Record the role and question, then predict the form. The rule checks your decision rather than replacing it.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the lesson and draw a three-object scene. Label roles without case names, then add questions and finally endings. Say the familiar sentence, a changed sentence, and your own. If a form disappears, return to the meaning arrow instead of mechanically trying every option.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner points at one map but says `карта үстелге қойыңызшы`. The word remains in naming form instead of being marked as the specific object of the instruction. Repair it to `картаны`, then remove the pointing gesture and compare the clarity.

## Transfer to a new setting

Receive an unseen scene with another object, person, route, or means. Explain the form with one short reason: ‘this is the goal’, ‘this is the location’, or ‘this is the source’. A partner changes one role; change only the ending and meaning connected with that role.

## Exercise
