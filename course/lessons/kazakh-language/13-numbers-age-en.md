# Counting people and rooms: numbers, age, and a short rhythm

_Lead (summary):_ **You will use one to ten, state age and quantity, and keep the noun singular after an exact number.**

## A real situation

A fictional home needs counts of rooms, chairs, and characters. We do not chant a detached number list; every number immediately performs a job in a meaningful sentence.

## How to practise

Listen with the text hidden and catch only the target meaning. Next, trace the map with a finger and repeat after the model. On the third pass, hide the model, tap its short rhythm with your palm, and make a sentence of your own. Device speech is a model; use a speaker, teacher, or comparison with your own recording as evidence of pronunciation.

## The precise model

Quantity follows `number + item`: `үш бөлме`, `төрт кітап`. The item does not take a plural ending after an exact number because quantity is already expressed. Age has its own frame: `Ол жеті жаста`; for oneself, `Мен он жастамын`. Keep ы/і in `бір`, `алты`, and `сегіз` short without adding an extra beat. Tap once per word rather than once per letter.

## The support signal

```kazakh
NUMBER + SINGULAR ITEM: ҮШ БӨЛМЕ | ОЛ + 7 + ЖАСТА | МЕН + 10 + ЖАСТАМЫН
```

## The limit of the rule

Do not request a real age when somebody would rather not share it; use a character for practice. `Үш бөлмелер` redundantly marks plurality in an ordinary quantity phrase. Numbers above ten are systematic, but today we automate one through ten.

## Predict before the explanation

Look at groups of three, four, and seven icons. Build `number + item` before reading, then predict where a learner may add a plural ending by habit.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the number line. Lay out ten objects, name each new count while removing one at a time, then recall three quantities and the ages of two fictional characters.

## Find and repair the mistake

Repair `үш бөлмелер`, `Ол жеті жас`, and `Мен он жаста`. The first duplicates plurality; the others leave the age or person frame incomplete. Explain the role of every part.

## Transfer to a new setting

Receive an unlabelled room plan. Count three object types in twenty seconds and report them. A partner changes one object; update only the affected number.

## Change to the My World project

Add a quantity legend and safe ages for two fictional characters to the project. Replace object groups with numerals, but keep one illustrated self-check version.

## Exercise

**Required.** Write ten short lines: five quantities and five ages of fictional characters. Underline each number and verify the noun form.

**Transfer check.** Report four quantities from an unfamiliar plan without preparation and correct one deliberately wrong count from a partner.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow retrieve 1–10 in random order.
- After seven days count a new room and state two ages.
- After thirty days give a one-minute description without adding plural endings after numbers.
