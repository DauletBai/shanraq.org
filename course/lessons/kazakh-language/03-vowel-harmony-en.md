# Vowel harmony: two sound families

_Lead (summary):_ **You will hear two Kazakh vowel families and choose `-лар` or `-лер` from the final vowel of the stem instead of memorising disconnected endings.**

## Where we are on the map

The map holds the whole route; we will add its details one at a time.

## A real situation

Picture a family table: a new suffix sits beside the stem and adjusts its vowel to the sound already there. That is why `бала` becomes `балалар` while `әже` becomes `әжелер`. The image opens the idea, but the exact decision comes from the final vowel of the stem.

## The precise model

For this first model, `а, о, ұ, ы` form the back-vowel family and `ә, ө, ү, і, е` form the front-vowel family. Find the last vowel in the stem. If it belongs to the first family, select a suffix with `а`: `бала + лар`. If it belongs to the second, select the `е` form: `әже + лер`. We do not try two endings at random; we hear the stem and preserve its sound family.

## The support signal

```kazakh
СОҢҒЫ ДАУЫСТЫ → ЖУАН: -ЛАР  |  ЖІҢІШКЕ: -ЛЕР
```

## The limit of the rule

Today we choose only the suffix vowel. The first consonant in `-лар/-лер`, `-дар/-дер`, and `-тар/-тер` depends on the final sound of the stem and needs a later rule. This one lesson therefore cannot derive all of `кітаптар`. Loanwords and established forms can also behave specially; check a dictionary when uncertain instead of forcing every word into the first diagram.

## Predict before the explanation

Without adding an ending, divide `ана`, `қала`, `үй`, `әже`, and `көше` into two families. Underline the final vowel, name the expected map colour, and only then say the plural form aloud.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the page and draw two houses. Rebuild `а о ұ ы` in the first and `ә ө ү і е` in the second. Draw an arrow from each house to `-лар` or `-лер`. Add two nouns of your own to each and justify the choice from the final vowel rather than your memory of a finished word.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner writes `үйлар` after taking the first ending from a table. Point to the actual decision: locate the last vowel in `үй`, name its family, and repair only the suffix vowel. Then explain why `балалер` contains the mirror-image error.

## Transfer to a new setting

Choose six nearby objects whose Kazakh names you know or have checked. Divide the stems into two families, then make plurals only where today's `-лар/-лер` model is sufficient. Put words that require another consonant variant aside with a note saying which part has not been learned yet.

## Change to the My World project

Add two safe lists of places or objects to the project: three back-vowel stems and three front-vowel stems. Store the plural beside each and colour only the changing suffix vowel. Add one sentence explaining why the colour carries meaning rather than decoration.

## Exercise

**Required.** Make plurals of `бала`, `ана`, `қала`, `әже`, `үй`, and `көше`; write the final vowel and its family before each answer. Read every pair aloud.

**Transfer check.** Find two new nouns for which today's rule is sufficient and one noun that must wait because of the consonant part of the suffix. Explain that boundary.
