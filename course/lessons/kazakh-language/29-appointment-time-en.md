# Booking a service: choosing and confirming a time

_Lead (summary):_ **You will state your goal, ask for an available time, offer an option, and repeat the booking so that its day and hour remain clear.**

## Where we are on the map

The support map presents an action as a short route: the situation cue, a useful line, the other person's response, and a result check. Trace the arrows and state the meaning of each node, then hide the map and rebuild it on a blank sheet. Colour helps you find a step, but the response must eventually work without colour.

## A real situation

You need an appointment at a library, service centre, or with a specialist. Several dates appear in the conversation, so you separate a requested time from the final confirmed time.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Мен қабылдауға жазылғым келеді.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-156.wav) — I would like to make an appointment.

[Listen: Бос уақыт бар ма?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-157.wav) — Is there an available time?

[Listen: Сейсенбі күні сағат үшке бола ма?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-158.wav) — Would Tuesday at three work?

[Listen: Иә, сағат үш бос.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-159.wav) — Yes, three o'clock is available.

[Listen: Менің атым — Аян.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-160.wav) — My name is Ayan.

[Listen: Сейсенбі, сағат үш, дұрыс па?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-161.wav) — Tuesday at three, is that correct?

## How to practise

On the first listen, hide the text and identify the place and the speaker's intention. On the second, reveal the Kazakh line, divide it into meaning units, and repeat after the recording without rushing. On the third, remove the model, change the time, product, service, or reason, and produce your own line. The recording models pronunciation; an appropriate response or action proves comprehension.

## The precise model

The intention appears in `жазылғым келеді`, ‘I would like to book’. An appointment time takes direction in `сағат үшке`: it is a slot towards which the visit is set. `Бос уақыт бар ма?` opens the available options. The final line repeats both day and time; `дұрыс` or a correction turns a proposal into a confirmed booking.

## The support signal

```kazakh
МАҚСАТ → БОС УАҚЫТ? → КҮН + САҒАТҚА → АТЫ → ҚАЙТАЛАУ → РАСТАУ
```

## The limit of the rule

A lesson line does not confirm a real booking; the organisation provides confirmation. Share only necessary details, and do not say document identifiers, medical information, or message codes in public. If you will be late or must cancel, use the organisation's official contact channel.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the model, inspect the situation and choose the line that would actually complete the task. Record your prediction. After the explanation, identify the meaning cue rather than an isolated ending: habit, sequence, quantity, request, appointment time, symptom, or detected error.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the page and draw the four main nodes of the support map in sixty seconds. Use the drawing to produce the familiar line, one changed line, and an appropriate reply. If a step disappears, reveal only that area and rebuild the chain once more.

## Find and repair the mistake

An operator offers Wednesday at four, but the learner repeats Tuesday at three. The booking cannot be treated as complete. Compare separate `КҮН` and `УАҚЫТ` cards, say both values, and wait for explicit confirmation.

## Transfer to a new setting

Change the life task itself: another morning, purchase, dish, time, institution, or problem. A partner supplies one unseen card and checks whether you clarify its meaning instead of reciting the practised dialogue.

## Change to the My World project

Add a fictional service calendar to the project: purpose, two available slots, selected time, and a confirmation mark. Do not include a real phone number or document number.

## Exercise

**Required.** Conduct two booking dialogues: one successful and one with a corrected time. In each, state the purpose, ask for an open slot, and repeat the result. **Transfer check.** A partner rejects the first option; suggest another without returning to a script.
