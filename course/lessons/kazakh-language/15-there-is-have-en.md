# What is in the home? Бар and жоқ instead of a translated ‘have’

_Lead (summary):_ **You will state what exists or is absent in a place or for a character, ask with бар ма, and answer without copying an English structure.**

## Where we are on the map

The support map shows one complete meaning link. Trace it with a finger, then rebuild the route without the picture.

![What is in the home? Бар and жоқ instead of a translated ‘have’](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-15-existence-en.svg)

## A real situation

A guest asks whether the home has a desk. Kazakh sets the scene, then the item, then the result: `Бөлмеде үстел бар` — there is a table in the room.

## How to practise

Listen with the text hidden and catch only the target meaning. Next, trace the map with a finger and repeat after the model. On the third pass, hide the model, tap its short rhythm with your palm, and make a sentence of your own. Device speech is a model; use a speaker, teacher, or comparison with your own recording as evidence of pronunciation.

## The precise model

The existence frame is `place/possessor + item + бар/жоқ`. `Бар` states presence; `жоқ` states absence. Add a separate question particle for `бар ма?` while preserving the basic order. With a person, the possessive phrase fills the scene role: `Менің інім бар` is organised roughly as “my younger brother exists”. This is a natural Kazakh construction, not a missing translation of have.

## The support signal

```kazakh
PLACE/POSSESSOR → ITEM → БАР / ЖОҚ | QUESTION: БАР МА?
```

## The limit of the rule

`Бар` has other meanings in other structures, including an imperative “go”; the sentence determines its role. Do not require disclosure about a real family or property. `Жоқ` needs no separate negative verb in this frame.

## Predict before the explanation

Show six plan cells, three occupied and three empty. Select `бар` or `жоқ` before listening, then turn two statements into questions.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the map and rebuild the order from three cards. A partner changes only the location or object; preserve the frame and update the answer.

## Find and repair the mistake

Repair `Мен бар кітап`, `Үйде жоқ үстел`, and `Барма?`. Restore the scene first, item second, and result last; write the question particle `ма` separately.

## Transfer to a new setting

Run an audio inventory: one learner sees a room and another sees a blank plan. The second learner reconstructs it through five `бар ма?` questions and marks uncertainty.

## Change to the My World project

Add a `бар/жоқ` layer to the plan with five statements and three questions. Use only fictional relationships and objects for characters.

## Exercise

**Required.** Write six statements and six `бар/жоқ` questions about a fictional home. Answers must vary a location or item as well as yes or no.

**Transfer check.** Rebuild an unfamiliar plan in no more than six questions and explain the word order in one line.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow rebuild scene, item, and result.
- After seven days run an inventory on a new plan.
- After thirty days describe the presence and absence of six objects without a text.
