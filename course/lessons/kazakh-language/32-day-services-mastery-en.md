# A1 mastery checkpoint: living a whole day without a script

_Lead (summary):_ **You will combine routine, sequence, shopping, ordering, booking, asking for help, and correcting an error in My World 1.0.**

## Where we are on the map

The support map presents an action as a short route: the situation cue, a useful line, the other person's response, and a result check. Trace the arrows and state the meaning of each node, then hide the map and rebuild it on a blank sheet. Colour helps you find a step, but the response must eventually work without colour.

![A1 mastery checkpoint: living a whole day without a script](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-32-mastery-en.svg)

## A real situation

A partner gives you an ordinary-day route with unexpected changes. You must understand the cards, complete tasks in a sound order, ask questions, and repair your response without a memorised monologue.

## How to practise

On the first listen, hide the text and identify the place and the speaker's intention. On the second, reveal the Kazakh line, divide it into meaning units, and repeat after the recording without rushing. On the third, remove the model, change the time, product, service, or reason, and produce your own line. The recording models pronunciation; an appropriate response or action proves comprehension.

## The precise model

Version 1.0 tests ten observable actions: a connected routine; four sequence cues; an exact quantity; a price question; a polite order and ingredient check; a booking that repeats day and time; a safe description of an observable sign; a specific error report; one repair question; and a changed response after an unseen card. Mastery requires 8 of 10, including transfer.

## The support signal

```kazakh
КҮН → РЕТ → ДҮКЕН → КАФЕ → ЖАЗЫЛУ → КӨМЕК → ҚАТЕ → ТЕКСЕРУ → 8/10
```

## The limit of the rule

The checkpoint measures completed tasks rather than performance style or speaking speed. A learner may pause and ask for repetition. Do not include real medical data, an address, documents, payment details, or a child's route. Course audio is a model rather than an automatic assessment of the learner's voice.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the model, inspect the situation and choose the line that would actually complete the task. Record your prediction. After the explanation, identify the meaning cue rather than an isolated ending: habit, sequence, quantity, request, appointment time, symptom, or detected error.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the page and draw the four main nodes of the support map in sixty seconds. Use the drawing to produce the familiar line, one changed line, and an appropriate reply. If a step disappears, reveal only that area and rebuild the chain once more.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner repeats six lines perfectly but continues to state the old total after a price changes. That is memory without comprehension. Stop the account, show the changed card, ask a clarification question, and rebuild only the affected step.

## Transfer to a new setting

Change the life task itself: another morning, purchase, dish, time, institution, or problem. A partner supplies one unseen card and checks whether you clarify its meaning instead of reciting the practised dialogue.

## Change to the My World project

Assemble My World 1.0: a safe daily timeline, four connectors, a shop list with a total, a menu, a booking calendar, a help card, and a form containing a learning error.

## Exercise

**Required.** Complete a five-minute unscripted route and meet 8 of 10 criteria, including an unseen card and independent repair. **Delayed check.** After seven days receive another sequence and meet 7 criteria without rereading the block.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow rebuild the support map and act out two lines without reading.
- After seven days complete a new everyday task and meet at least 7 of 10 criteria.
- After thirty days use the construction in a safe real setting and mark the support you no longer need.
