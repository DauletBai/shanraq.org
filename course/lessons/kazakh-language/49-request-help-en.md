# Ask for help and name the action

_Lead (summary):_ **You will ask for specific help and check whether your partner has agreed.**

## Where we are on the map

This support map leads from a situation to a solution: notice the need or obstacle, choose a precise utterance, hear the reply, and check that both people understand the outcome alike. Trace the path with a finger, hide the map, and rebuild it from memory. Each node serves a real exchange rather than the memorisation of a rule name.

![Ask for help and name the action](/static/course/kazakh-language/map-49-request-en.svg)

## A real situation

Your group is preparing a neighbourhood map. One symbol is unclear and your partner has found the key. A bare ‘help me’ does not say what to do.

## Listen and respond

[Listen: Маған көмектесе аласыз ба?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-275.wav) — Could you help me?

[Listen: Мына белгіні түсіндіріп бере аласыз ба?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-276.wav) — Could you explain this symbol?

[Listen: Иә, бірге қарайық.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-277.wav) — Yes, let us look together.

[Listen: Қазір уақытым жоқ, кейін көмектесемін.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-278.wav) — I cannot now; I can help later.

[Listen: Қай белгі түсініксіз?](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-279.wav) — Which symbol is unclear?

[Listen: Рақмет, енді түсіндім.](/static/course/kazakh-language/audio/kz-280.wav) — Thank you, I understand now.

## How to practise

First play the recording without reading the Kazakh line. Decide whether the speaker is requesting, replying, explaining, or confirming. Then uncover the line, repeat it as a whole after the speaker, and point out the word that changes the meaning. On the third pass, alter one condition and say your own line. If a partner understands it differently, ask for clarification; that is a communication skill.

## The precise model

A polite request joins the beneficiary `маған`, the action `түсіндіріп беру`, and the listener's ability `аласыз ба?`. Point to or name the object first: `мына белгіні`. Confirm what you understood after the reply. ‘Later’ is not consent to help right now.

## The support signal

```kazakh
НЫСАН → ӨТІНІШ → ЖАУАП → РАСТАУ
```

## The limit of the rule

Use `беріңізші` for handing over an object and `түсіндіріп бере аласыз ба?` for an explanation. Do not treat a busy person's delay as a refusal or a promise.

## Predict before the explanation

Before reading the model, write your first response to the situation. Which facts are known and which are guesses? Choose an action supported by what you know. After the explanation, compare not only word endings but also whether the request or agreement kept its intended meaning.

## Rebuild it without the prompt

Hide the page. In one minute draw the four nodes, say a familiar line, the partner's reply, and your answer. Then change one fact such as time, place, or available object and travel through the path again without the example.

## Find and repair the mistake

A learner says only `маған көмектесіңіз` and expects the partner to guess the task. Add object and action: `Мына белгіні түсіндіріп бере аласыз ба?` Then wait for the answer.

## Transfer to a new setting

Ask about an unseen symbol on a different diagram; your partner either agrees or offers a later time.

## Change to the My World project

Add a request card to My World 1.3: an unclear map symbol, a precise question, the reply, and an ‘understood’ mark.

## Exercise

Role-play two requests: explain a symbol and show a route. Paraphrase each reply. Change roles and the object of the request.

## Retrieval after 1, 7, and 30 days

- Tomorrow rebuild the four nodes and speak a new two-line exchange without reading.
- After seven days solve an unseen task with a partner; they must restate your final plan in their own words.
- After thirty days return to the project card, change one condition, and repair an unclear utterance yourself.
