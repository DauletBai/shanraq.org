# How do we read H₂O, CO₂ and 2H₂O?

_Лид (summary):_ **Separate an element symbol, subscript and coefficient before counting atoms.**

## Situation and question

H₂O and 2H₂O are on the board. The digit two appears in both, but means different things. How can we avoid a counting mistake?

## New terms, fully explained

An **element symbol** names an element: H is hydrogen, O oxygen, C carbon. A **subscript** applies to a symbol or group inside one formula: H₂O has two H and one O. A **coefficient** before a formula counts whole molecules or formula units: 2H₂O represents two water molecules, four H and two O atoms in all. A missing subscript means one. A formula states composition; by itself it shows neither molecular shape nor whether a reaction occurred.

## Predict first

Before reading the example, count atoms of each element in three CO₂ molecules. What do the leading 3 and the subscript ₂ after O each mean?

## Investigate or analyse given data

Make a table with rows H₂O, CO₂, 2H₂O and 3CO₂. For each, separately record particle count and counts of H, C and O. Use pencil and paper cards only; do not produce any gas.

## Worked example

3CO₂ means three molecules, each with one C and two O, so three C and six O in all. 2H₂O means two molecules, four H and two O. For NaCl, count formula units rather than molecules: 2NaCl gives a ratio of two Na⁺ to two Cl⁻ in the corresponding part of a crystal.

## A common mistake

**Mistake:** reading 2H₂O as one H₄O₂ molecule. The total atom count matches, but the molecule count and composition are misrepresented. Do not apply a coefficient to the first element only. A list of formulas does not prove a reaction happened.

## Transfer to a new case

Decode 4H₂O and 2CO₂ without hints. How many molecules of each kind, and how many oxygen atoms in each set?

## Answer and recall

**Check:** 4H₂O means four molecules and four O; 2CO₂ means two molecules and four O. Equal oxygen counts do not make the substances identical. Invent a case where the subscript and coefficient lead to different counts.

## Knowledge check

1. How many O atoms in 3CO₂?
2. How many molecules in 2H₂O?

**Answers:** Six O; two molecules.

## When to revisit

Close the page and, after 10 minutes, reconstruct the map, define the terms in your own words and solve the new case. Repeat without hints after a day and a week. If an error persists, return to the worked example and write down the missing step.
