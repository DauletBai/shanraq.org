A **motion report** states its reference point, direction, original positions and time readings, calculated intervals, graph, and a conclusion with limits. Label the numbers below as worked data if you use them. A real trial with a light toy cart and measuring tape is only suitable on a clear level surface, away from people and fragile objects.

## Work through the numbers

Worked pairs (t, x): (0 s, 0 m), (2 s, 2 m), (4 s, 4 m), (6 s, 6 m). Position changes by +6 m in 6 s. If the cart did not reverse, distance is 6 m and average speed is 6/6 = 1 m/s. The points lie on a line consistent with steady motion. Sparse observations cannot prove there were no brief stops between them.

## Your paper investigation

Submit seven parts: question, data source, axis sketch with zero, table with units, one calculation with units, labelled graph, and conclusion with a limit. Can a friend obtain 1 m/s without prompting? If the origin of 6 m is unclear, improve the report.

## An easy mistake to make

Do not turn worked numbers into “I measured.” A straight line through sparse observations does not prove constant instantaneous speed between observations.
