# How can a small piston move a larger load?

_Лид (summary):_ **Understand Pascal’s principle, force gain and the shorter travel that pays for it. No needles or high pressure.**

## Story and prediction

Two connected, water-filled needle-free syringes can have different piston areas. Pressing the small piston gently can yield a larger force at the big one. But the big piston moves a shorter distance. Energy has not been created for free: the fluid transmits pressure while the volume displaced must be accounted for. Before calculating, predict which piston travels farther.

Before reading, put your prediction in your notebook and justify it in one sentence. After calculating, return to it: a result matching your guess does not yet prove your explanation. Check which conditions were supplied and which things you actually observed.

## Words to learn

**Pascal’s principle** says a change in pressure in a confined stationary liquid is transmitted through it. For ideal pistons at the same height, p = F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂. **Hydraulics** uses liquid to transmit an action. For an incompressible liquid, **displaced volume** gives A₁s₁ = A₂s₂, where s is piston travel. Seals, friction, air bubbles and height difference affect real results; our equations describe an ideal model.

## Check the model with numbers

Take A₁ = 2 cm², A₂ = 10 cm² and F₁ = 4 N. The area ratio is 5, so F₂ = 20 N. If the small piston travels 5 cm, the big one moves only 1 cm: 2 × 5 = 10 × 1 cm³. Work check: 4 N × 0.05 m = 0.2 J, while 20 N × 0.01 m = 0.2 J. A real device delivers less because of losses.

## A safe investigation

Sketch piston faces with 2 and 10 grid squares. Draw travel arrows of 5 and 1 squares. If you have a ready-made set of needle-free syringes, use water only, with adult supervision, no high pressure and no hard pushing. Without it, calculate the force for areas 3 and 6 cm² with a 3 N input: 6 N.

## A likely mistake

Trap: “five times more force means five times more energy”. No; the larger piston travels one fifth as far. Never try to lift a real heavy load with a homemade device.
