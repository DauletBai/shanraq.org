## A scene from everyday life

A teacher says, “Class starts at 9:00 and ends at 9:45.” The first two values are instants; 45 minutes is the duration. In an experiment a stopwatch may read 2 s when the cart passes one mark and 7 s at the next. “Time = 7 s” without more detail would be ambiguous.

## New words, step by step

An **instant** t is a clock reading in an agreed time system. A **time interval** Δt is the duration between two instants: Δt = t₂ − t₁. We measure it in seconds, minutes, or hours. The stopwatch’s zero does not have to coincide with the start of the cart’s movement.

## Work through the numbers

The cart passes A at t₁ = 2 s and B at t₂ = 7 s. The interval is Δt = 7 − 2 = 5 s. Check: 2 + 5 = 7. These readings alone do not reveal its speed on every tiny part of the track.

## Your paper investigation

Draw a time line and mark 2 and 7 s. Shade the stretch between them: it represents 5 s, not 7 s. Invent another experiment starting at 12 s and lasting the same 5 s. It ends at 17 s.

## An easy mistake to make

A common error is dividing the track length by the final stopwatch reading, 7 s. If timing began earlier, use the 5 s interval.
