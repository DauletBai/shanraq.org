# Where does a measurement begin?

_Лид (summary):_ **Write a procedure someone else can repeat.**

## Question and prediction

Two learners measured one cart run. One placed the ruler zero at the ramp exit, the other at the table edge. Their numbers differ though the cart is unchanged. They must agree on start and end points before measuring. Without them, numerical comparisons are meaningless.

## New words without gaps

The zero mark starts the horizontal run. The end mark is the front axle’s position when the cart has fully stopped. Another reference point works if used consistently. A scale division is the gap between adjacent ruler marks. A ruler marked every 1 cm does not justify claiming hundredths of a centimetre. A repeat is a fresh release, not rereading the same stop.

## Work through supplied data

The supplied paper trials are 82, 84, 83 cm. Table headings: surface; trial; distance, cm; note. If a broken zero is replaced with the 2 cm mark and the cart stops at 84 cm, the distance is 84 − 2 = 82 cm. The number 84 is a position on a scale, not the distance travelled.

## Test safely

Write exact instructions for someone else: secure a low ramp; mark release height and zero; cover only the horizontal run; release without a push; mark the axle at rest; read the ruler at eye level; record a number and unit. Mark and repeat a disturbed trial instead of silently erasing it.

## A common mistake

Mistake: moving the origin between series. Transfer: the starting ruler mark is 5 cm and the final mark is 50 cm. What is the run? Why is it 45 cm, not 50 cm?
