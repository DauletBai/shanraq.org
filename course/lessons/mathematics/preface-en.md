# How we will learn mathematics: from memorizing to understanding

_Lead (summary):_ **The course follows a dependency map: every new step rests on an earlier idea, and understanding is checked through explanation and transfer to a new situation.**

## Where we are on the map

This is the map of the whole route. After diagnosis, we move through numbers, fractions, prealgebra, and functions in a deliberate order.

![Map of the mathematics course from numbers to algebra and modeling](/static/course/mathematics/map-full-en.svg)

## Begin with a familiar image

A house is built from its foundation, then its walls, and only then its upper floors. Mathematics works the same way: fractions depend on the idea of number, while equations depend on the meaning of operations.

## Precise meaning

Topics are ordered by what they require, rather than by a school grade label. A mistake is useful evidence: it points to the support idea that needs to be rebuilt.

## The lesson's support signal

`meaning → model → calculation → check → explain in your own words`

image → compact support → explain in your own words → apply → recall later

## Worked example

If a learner cannot solve a percentage problem, we do not begin with another formula. We first check whether one hundredth, fractions, and decimal notation are understood.

## Example with a fading prompt

Change the numbers in the example and repeat the solution without copying its steps. Estimate first, calculate exactly, and check against the original condition.

## Recall without a prompt

1. Define the main idea in one sentence.
2. Rebuild the support signal from memory.
3. Solve the example with different numbers and explain why every step is valid.

## Find and correct the mistake

The claim “I read the topic, so I know it” is unreliable. Evidence of learning is explaining without the page, solving with new numbers, and correcting an error.

## Transfer to a new setting

Apply the same relationship to something you can measure yourself. Name the quantities, units, and the boundary within which the model is valid. Check the answer by a second representation or an inverse operation.

## Exercise

**Required.** Study the map and name three ideas that feel secure and three that feel uncertain. Complete the diagnosis honestly and mark the first missing support.

**With your own data.** Create one everyday example with the same structure and show it in words, a formula, and a check.

**Optional.** Seven days later, change the numbers and repeat without the support map.
