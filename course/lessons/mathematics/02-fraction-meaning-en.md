# A fraction is a number, not two numbers stacked together

_Lead (summary):_ **We connect part of a whole, measurement, division, and a point on a line so numerator and denominator gain precise meaning.**

## Where we are on the map

We move from whole numbers to fractional numbers below and above one.

![Three of eight equal parts and the point 3/8 on a number line](/static/course/mathematics/map-02-fraction-en.svg)

## Begin with a familiar image

Cutting one pizza into eight equal pieces and taking three gives `3/8`. Equal parts are essential.

## Precise meaning

The denominator tells how many equal parts make the whole; the numerator tells how many are selected. `a/b` is also the number `a ÷ b`.

## The lesson's support signal

`meaning → model → calculation → check → explain in your own words`

unit → equal parts → size of one part → number of selected parts

## Worked example

For `3/8`, divide one unit into eight equal parts and take three; its point lies between 0 and 1, three eighths from zero.

## Example with a fading prompt

Change the numbers in the example and repeat the solution without copying its steps. Estimate first, calculate exactly, and check against the original condition.

## Recall without a prompt

1. Define the main idea in one sentence.
2. Rebuild the support signal from memory.
3. Solve the example with different numbers and explain why every step is valid.

## Find and correct the mistake

It is wrong to call `3/8` and `3/5` equal because their numerators match: a fifth is larger than an eighth.

## Transfer to a new setting

Apply the same relationship to something you can measure yourself. Name the quantities, units, and the boundary within which the model is valid. Check the answer by a second representation or an inverse operation.

## Exercise

**Required.** A tank holds 12 equal buckets and already contains 7. Represent the filled amount as a fraction, a division, and a point on a line.

**With your own data.** Create one everyday example with the same structure and show it in words, a formula, and a check.

**Optional.** Seven days later, change the numbers and repeat without the support map.
